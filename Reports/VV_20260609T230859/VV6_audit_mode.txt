V&V6 — Audit mode

Open sim_v2.html and use the Analytics sidebar (A key) to inspect any
policy timeline event-by-event. The JSONL files at
  output/sim_timeline_<policy>.jsonl
expose every order_start / op_grant / op_move / rt_dispatch / pick /
kardex_pick / order_done event with sim-time t in minutes.

Step-through: use ⏮/⏭ buttons (30 sim-min jumps) or scrubber.
Pick events flash a 2-second yellow highlight on the target rack-bay.
KPI snapshots every 10 sim-min drive the HUD.
